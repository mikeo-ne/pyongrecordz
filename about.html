About Pyong

The Story

From Ntinda, With Love

Pyong Recordz grew out of Ntinda, Kampala — founded by Pryce Teeba after a career shaped at Dustville Records. It represents a new legacy: a home for raw, honest Ugandan hip-hop built on two decades of grind.

From the mixtapes to Marinate — the label's definitive 14-track album featuring Uganda's hip-hop legends — Pyong Recordz keeps its roots planted in the streets that raised it.
